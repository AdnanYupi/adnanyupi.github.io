About

I'm a senior Android and iOS developer with around 10 years of experience in native mobile app development. I build Android apps in Android Studio with Kotlin and Java, and iOS apps in Xcode with Swift, SwiftUI and Storyboards. I've also built apps for Wear OS smartwatches.

I work with the MVVM architecture. Tools and libraries I've used in my projects include:

  • Android: Jetpack Compose, Material Design, Kotlin Coroutines and Flow, Hilt, Kodein, RxJava, LiveData, ViewBinding and DataBinding, Room, Realm, Retrofit2, Volley.
  • iOS: SwiftUI, Alamofire, Kingfisher.
  • Services and SDKs: REST APIs, Ktor, AWS, Firebase (Authentication, Database, Storage), Parse, AdMob, Google Maps (current location, navigation), Bluetooth.
  • Version control: Git on GitHub, Bitbucket and GitLab.
